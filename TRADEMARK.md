# Noiro working-name and brand policy

Noiro is the working product name and **has not yet passed formal name or
trademark clearance**. Noirvo is the only approved fallback if clearance blocks
Noiro. No public sale, listing, or trademark claim may be made before written
clearance is recorded in the release approvals.

The source code is GPL-3.0. That license grants the rights to use, study, modify,
and redistribute the covered code, including commercially. It does not grant a
right to use the Prismatic N artwork or a future cleared Noiro mark in a way
that suggests an official build.

A redistributed fork must preserve GPL notices and corresponding-source duties,
use its own identifiers and signing material, and avoid implying endorsement.
Factual attribution and references to upstream history remain permitted.
